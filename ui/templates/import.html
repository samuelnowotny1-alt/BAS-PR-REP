Upload schedules and supporting engineering artifacts into the persisted ingestion pipeline

Equipment Schedule

Required columns: Equipment ID, Equipment Type. Optional: Subtype, Building, Floor, Room, Served Area, Controller ID, Parent/Child Equipment, Points, Design CFM/Tonnage/GPM/kW, Voltage, Phase, Status, Graphic Sections, Tags, Notes

Point List

Required columns: Point Name, Equipment ID, Point Kind, Direction. Optional: Units, Range Min/Max, Controller ID, BACnet/Modbus config, Source, Description, Tags

Controller Schedule

Required columns: Controller ID. Optional: Name, Vendor, Model, Firmware, Type, Protocols, IP Address, Panel Location, Serves Equipment, Owned Points, I/O Capacity, Status, Notes

Supporting Documents
