# Contributing

This repository is not currently open for unsolicited public contributions.

Do not submit code, content, assets, documentation, or other material unless
you have written authorization from the project owner.

If you are invited to contribute, make sure these conditions are settled before
work is merged:

1. The contribution is original to you or you have documented permission to
submit it.
2. Any third-party code, datasets, fonts, images, or templates are identified
with their source and license terms.
3. Ownership and licensing terms are covered by a separate written agreement,
such as a contractor IP assignment or contributor agreement.

Until a formal contributor agreement process is in place, maintainers may
decline contributions for ownership or licensing reasons alone.
